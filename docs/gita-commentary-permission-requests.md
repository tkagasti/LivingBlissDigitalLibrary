# Bhagavad Gita commentary permission requests

Prepared for Living Bliss Digital Library on 15 September 2026.

## Rights requested

Living Bliss requests a non-exclusive licence to:

- reproduce and store the complete commentary in its own database;
- display it publicly at `library.livingbliss.org`, aligned to the relevant Bhagavad Gita chapter and verse;
- preserve the text locally so the service is not dependent on the source website;
- create and display faithful script conversions and authorised translations in Sanskrit, English, Hindi, and Odia;
- index the text for site search and retrieval-augmented educational chat, without using it to train a general-purpose AI model;
- retain the content in backups and preservation archives;
- identify the author, edition, publisher, rights holder, and any wording of attribution required by the licensor.

The request should ask the rights holder to specify any fee, attribution, territorial, duration, editing, translation, or takedown conditions.

Living Bliss is developing an educational digital library at library.livingbliss.org for systematic study of all eighteen chapters of the Shreemad Bhagavad Gita. We request a non-exclusive licence covering database storage, public educational display, chapter-and-verse indexing, local preservation, search, and retrieval-augmented educational chat. We would also like permission to create faithful script conversions and authorised translations in Sanskrit, English, Hindi, and Odia. The material would not be used to train a general-purpose AI model.

Living Bliss will preserve all required author, edition, publisher, institutional, and copyright attributions. We are willing to follow reasonable display, linking, correction, takedown, and version-control conditions. Please advise whether IIT Kanpur controls these rights or whether separate approval is required from individual publishers or rights holders, and please provide the appropriate licence terms or agreement.

With respect,

Living Bliss Digital Library
